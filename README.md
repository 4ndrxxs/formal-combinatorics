# Formal combinatorics in Lean

The nine projects below each address a specific question in the cited mathematical literature. Each has its own English proof statement, Lean sources and build instructions.

1. [Kuperberg’s Conjecture 5.1 on modular Hadamard orders](https://github.com/4ndrxxs/kuperberg-modular-hadamard)
2. [Problem 15 on the determinants of three near-Hadamard families](https://github.com/4ndrxxs/near-hadamard-problem-15)
3. [Cardinalities of quantum Latin squares](https://github.com/4ndrxxs/quantum-latin-square-spectrum)
4. [Nechita–Pillet Conjecture 6 on quantum Sudoku](https://github.com/4ndrxxs/quantum-sudoku-existence-conjecture)
5. [Weber’s Problem 3.5 on projection splittings](https://github.com/4ndrxxs/weber-projection-splittings)
6. [Weber’s Problem 3.6 on quantum permutation completion](https://github.com/4ndrxxs/weber-quantum-permutation-completion)
7. [Kimberling–Moses Conjecture 6 on complementary sequences](https://github.com/4ndrxxs/kimberling-moses-conjecture-6)
8. [Kimberling–Moses Conjecture 14 on the offset by two](https://github.com/4ndrxxs/kimberling-moses-conjecture-14)
9. [Chen’s question on universal perfect edge transfer](https://github.com/4ndrxxs/universal-bipartite-edge-transfer)

## Statements and other results

The [problem guide](by-problem.md) gives each original question, the exact result and the primary Lean source.

The [research archive](archive/README.md) contains the other results, including bounds, constructions and special cases. The [original source snapshot](https://github.com/4ndrxxs/formal-combinatorics/tree/1024b8d59eca29300691c87cb92c1c1fa51940c6) preserves the collection in its initial layout.
