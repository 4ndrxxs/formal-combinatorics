# Twelve-cell Hessian discriminants

Let a size-$m$ affine determinant represent the permanent of order five over a characteristic-zero field $F$. In the normalized pencil, $h=m-1$ and $L$ is the kernel of the two constant-coefficient endpoint maps. At every centre over the field of definition there exists a subspace $J_y$ of $L$ with dimension at least $25-h$ on which every Hessian pairing vanishes. If $m$ is at most twenty, this supplies six independent isotropic directions.

A coordinate degeneration of $L$ has a pivot support $S$. For any twelve-cell board $T$ containing $S$, replace the torus parameter in the pivot paths by its square and extend them by the unit directions indexed by $T$ outside $S$. Their values at zero are the coordinate directions of $T$ and their first derivatives vanish. Over $F(t)$ the inverse torus transform of the pivot paths spans the entire kernel of the two original endpoint maps after scalar extension. The kernel is independent of the centre.

Take a polynomial centre $Y(t)=y_0+t v$ and apply the isotropic-space theorem over $F(t)$ at the inverse torus transform of $Y(t)$. The twelve pulled-back directions span a space containing the resulting six-dimensional isotropic subspace. Their Gram determinant is a square. The torus multiplies this determinant by a twelfth power. Thus the Gram determinant before pullback is also a square in $F(t)$. Modulo $t^2$ it agrees with the coordinate Gram determinant on $T$. An integral certificate giving constant term zero and nonzero linear coefficient yields valuation one and rules out the proposed pencil.

The file `twelve-block-data.json` indexes cells by $5i+j$. Each of the nine records gives a binary centre, one varying cell, a kernel vector, a nonzero principal minor of order eleven, and the derivative on the kernel vector. The script `check_quintic_discriminant.py` reconstructs all matrices by differentiating the 120 permutation monomials. It also checks four rank-eight ambient minors at the thirteen-cell indicator matrices. The script `check_quintic_layout.py` supplies row and column permutations for the 52 possible single deletions of those four boards. The Lean files check each identity and containment independently.

For thirteen cells, $26+\operatorname{rank}H\le m+13$ and the rank-eight witness imply $m\ge21$. For fourteen or fifteen cells, the rank-ten witness implies $m\ge36-|S|\ge21$. The theorem `PermanentFive.determinantal_lower_bound_twenty_one` combines the three size cases over every field of characteristic zero.
